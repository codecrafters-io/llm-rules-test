---

## Rule
- The learner sees three scopes. The *challenge* is the whole challenge, an *extension* is one group of stages within it, and a *stage* is the current step.
- Do not use other names for them, such as "course", "exercise", or "lesson".
- Use the word that matches the scope: something that spans an extension's stages is "in this extension", not "in this challenge".
- The verb "exercise" ("later cases exercise this path") and the phrase "of course" are fine.

## Good examples
- "In this extension, you'll add support for lists."
- "The tester will create the invoice project."

## Bad examples
- "In this course, you'll build a shell."
- "Run the exercise with `codecrafters test`."
- "This lesson covers the `PING` command."

## How to fix
- Replace "course" with "challenge", and "exercise" or "lesson" with "stage", or name the thing directly ("the invoice project").
